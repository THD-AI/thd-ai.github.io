02 / Why this exists

Good work deserves
a little company.

Some projects start in a lecture. Some start with a small annoyance, a late-night idea, or a question you can't leave alone. They deserve a life beyond a submission folder.

THD AI is an independent place to show that work and meet people who care about it. For anyone connected to Deggendorf Institute of Technology with curiosity and a love for AI, software, and making things.

You choose your own project, pace, and collaborators. AI-assisted or made without AI, the important things are care, quality, honest credit, and something worth sharing.

03 / Leave something on the shelf

Your project.
Your next people.

It doesn't need to be perfect. Give it a clear description, show what works, credit the people and sources behind it, and tell us what kind of company you're looking for.

Keep your repository where it is. A reviewed submission adds a link here.
Organization members can also create and lead repositories of their own.

  1. 01

    Show us the idea.

    Link a public repository, explain what it does, and name its creators.

  2. 02

    Make it easy to explore.

    A screenshot, a demo, or clear setup instructions gives someone a way in.

  3. 03

    Say what comes next.

    Feedback? A collaborator? Someone to take it further? Tell people.
