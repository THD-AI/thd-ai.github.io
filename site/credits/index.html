The showroom connects projects. It does not take ownership of them. Every project keeps its original creators, repository, and licence.

Campus artwork

The public campus banner adapts the campus illustration from THD Room Finder. Its source photograph is Hauptcampus der Technischen Hochschule Deggendorf 2018 by Danube-Waidler, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The repainted illustration and public banner are shared under that same licence. The banner widens the composition, changes the light and palette, simplifies people into grey silhouettes, and adds THD AI lettering.

The member-workspace and showcase illustrations follow the same visual family, using the Room Finder art as visual references. These new illustrations are also shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The original public banner remains unchanged. Detailed source notes and production briefs live in the brand record.

The open-portal symbol

The THD AI symbol is an open architectural portal with a warm sun at its centre: room for a person, an idea, and what comes next. The symbol is community identity artwork shared under CC BY 4.0. No official university marks are used.

Project images and creators

  • THD Room Finder: Alex Rudaev. The study-space illustration is taken from its original public repository; the project is GPL-3.0. Consult the project's artwork notes for asset-specific terms.

Image source URLs are recorded in asset-sources.json. Historical visuals retain their original rights and are not relicensed by appearing in the archive.

Website

The original website code and documentation are MIT-licensed. This does not relicense the images or linked projects. The typefaces are Manrope and DM Sans, served by Google Fonts, with system sans-serif fallbacks. The site has no analytics, visitor accounts, or advertising.
