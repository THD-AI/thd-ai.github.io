# THD AI – project showroom

An independent place for people around Deggendorf Institute of Technology to share personal and university projects, find inspiration, and collaborate in their own time.

Website: https://thd-ai.github.io/

The showroom source and contribution workflows live here. Original project repositories, maintainers, and licences remain with their creators.

The public showroom is a small selection, currently featuring THD Room Finder. Course projects and learning resources remain available in the [course directory](https://github.com/THD-AI/.github/blob/main/COURSES.md); they are not automatically showcased.
